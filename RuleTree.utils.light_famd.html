Bases: BaseEstimator, TransformerMixin

Correspondence Analysis of a contingency table.

Parameters:

  • n_components (int, default=2) – Number of components.

  • n_iter (int, default=10) – Number of iterations of the randomized SVD.

  • copy (bool, default=True) – Whether to copy the data.

  • check_input (bool, default=True) – Whether to check the input.

  • random_state (int, default=None) – Seed of the SVD.

  • engine ({'auto', 'sklearn', 'fbpca'}, default='auto') – SVD implementation.
