BivariateTransformer: oblique directions on the best pair of features.

class BivariateTransformer(ml_task='clf', n_orientations=10, tree_params=None)[source]#

Bases: ObliqueTransformer

Transformer into oblique directions on a pair of features: cos(a) * X_i + sin(a) * X_j for n_orientations angles a between 0 and 180 degrees.

The pair is chosen in fit: for each pair of numerical features, a depth-1 decision tree of the task is fitted on its projections, and the pair with the highest gain is kept. The splitter then chooses the angle and the threshold. The projections are computed on the features as they are: scale the data beforehand if the features have different scales.

Parameters:

  • ml_task ({MODEL_TYPE_CLF, MODEL_TYPE_REG}, default=MODEL_TYPE_CLF) – Task of the stump, which determines the trees that choose the pair.

  • n_orientations (int, default=10) – Number of angles, evenly spaced in [0, 180) degrees.

  • tree_params (dict, default=None) – Parameters of the depth-1 decision trees that choose the pair (e.g. criterion, random_state).

Variables:

  • numerical (list of int) – Numerical columns of the feature matrix.

  • categorical (list of int) – Categorical columns of the feature matrix (none).

  • num_pre_transformed (list of int) – Numerical columns of the data.

  • coefficients (numpy.ndarray of shape (n_numerical_features, n_orientations)) – Coefficients of each direction: non-zero only on the chosen pair (only the chosen direction after select).

See also

oblique directions from a reflection of the principal component.

RuleTree.stumps.transformers.HouseholderTransformer module#

HouseholderTransformer: oblique directions from the Householder reflection of the principal component.

class HouseholderTransformer(max_oblique_features=2, tau=0.0001, random_state=None)[source]#

Bases: ObliqueTransformer

Transformer into the oblique directions given by a Householder reflection: the reflection that maps the principal component of the instances of the node onto the closest axis.

The reflected axes H = I - 2 u u^T are the columns of the feature matrix: the splitter chooses the best threshold on one of them. The reflection vector u is restricted to the max_oblique_features features with the largest weights, so that each oblique direction combines at most that many features (plus the axis itself); the axes not involved in the reflection stay axis-parallel. The directions are computed on the numerical features of the node, as they are: scale the data beforehand if the features have different scales.

Parameters:

  • max_oblique_features (int, default=2) – Maximum number of features in the reflection vector.

  • tau (float, default=1e-4) – If the principal component is closer than tau to every axis, the directions are the axes (no reflection).

  • random_state (int, RandomState instance or None, default=None) – Seed of the randomized solver of the PCA, used on large nodes.

Variables:

  • numerical (list of int) – Numerical columns of the feature matrix.

  • categorical (list of int) – Categorical columns of the feature matrix (none).

  • num_pre_transformed (list of int) – Numerical columns of the data.

  • coefficients (numpy.ndarray of shape (n_numerical_features, n_directions)) – The columns of the reflection H (only the chosen one after select).

See also

BivariateTransformer

oblique directions on pairs of features.

ObliqueDecisionTreeStumpClassifier, ObliqueDecisionTreeStumpRegressor

Examples

>>> from sklearn.datasets import load_iris
>>> from RuleTree.stumps.transformers import HouseholderTransformer
>>> X, y = load_iris(return_X_y=True)
>>> transformer = HouseholderTransformer().fit(X, y)
>>> transformer.transform(X).shape  # one column per reflected axis
(150, 4)
>>> features, coefficients = transformer.combination(1)  # the reflected axis of feature 1
>>> features, [round(c, 3) for c in coefficients]
([1, 2], [-0.085, 0.857])

MultiplePivotTransformer: which of two pivots is closer to each instance.

class MultiplePivotTransformer(ml_task='clf', distance_measure='euclidean', k=None, reg_contrast_lambda=0.2, reg_max_thresholds=None, tree_params=None, discriminative='tree', max_oblique_features=2, tau=0.0001, n_orientations=10)[source]#

Bases: PivotTransformer

Transformer of tabular data into pivot comparisons: for each pair of candidate pivots, whether the instance is closer to the second one than to the first.

The candidate pivots are selected as in PivotTransformer (with discriminative, also with oblique directions); for classification, only the pairs of pivots of different classes are compared. The feature matrix has one 0/1 column for each pair (see closer_to_second()). After the splitter has chosen a pair, only its two pivots are kept (select).

Parameters:

  • ml_task ({MODEL_TYPE_CLF, MODEL_TYPE_REG}, default=MODEL_TYPE_CLF) – Task of the stump, which determines how the pivots are selected and compared.

  • distance_measure (str or callable, default='euclidean') – Distance between the instances, computed on the standardized numerical features.

  • k – Regression only: see PivotTransformer.

  • reg_contrast_lambda – Regression only: see PivotTransformer.

  • reg_max_thresholds – Regression only: see PivotTransformer.

  • tree_params (dict, default=None) – Parameters of the decision trees that select the discriminative pivots.

  • discriminative ({'tree', 'householder', 'bivariate'}, default='tree') – How the discriminative pivots are found (see PivotSelection).

  • max_oblique_features (int, default=2) – Householder only: maximum number of discriminative pivots of each class.

  • tau (float, default=1e-4) – Householder only: threshold of the reflection.

  • n_orientations (int, default=10) – Bivariate only: number of angles.

Variables:

  • numerical (list of int) – Numerical columns of the feature matrix.

  • categorical (list of int) – Categorical columns of the feature matrix (none).

  • scaler (StandardScaler) – Scaler of the numerical features, fitted on the whole data set.

ObliquePivotTransformer: oblique directions on the distances from the candidate pivots.

class ObliquePivotTransformer(ml_task='clf', distance_measure='euclidean', k=None, reg_contrast_lambda=0.2, reg_max_thresholds=None, tree_params=None, oblique_split_type='householder', max_oblique_features=2, tau=0.0001, n_orientations=10)[source]#

Bases: PivotTransformer

Transformer of tabular data into oblique directions on the distances from the candidate pivots, e.g. 0.7 * P_12 - 0.3 * P_57 where P_i is the distance from the training instance i.

The candidate pivots are selected as in PivotTransformer, but the discriminative ones are the instances combined by an oblique direction (oblique_split_type, see PivotSelection). Then the distances of the instances of the node from the candidates are transformed by an oblique transformer of the same type (HouseholderTransformer or BivariateTransformer), whose directions are the columns of the feature matrix. After the splitter has chosen a column, only its direction is kept (select); the candidates are all kept.

Parameters:

  • ml_task ({MODEL_TYPE_CLF, MODEL_TYPE_REG}, default=MODEL_TYPE_CLF) – Task of the stump, which determines how the pivots and the directions are selected.

  • distance_measure (str or callable, default='euclidean') – Distance between the instances, computed on the standardized numerical features.

  • k – Regression only: see PivotTransformer.

  • reg_contrast_lambda – Regression only: see PivotTransformer.

  • reg_max_thresholds – Regression only: see PivotTransformer.

  • tree_params (dict, default=None) – Parameters of the decision trees that select the pivots and the directions.

  • oblique_split_type ({'householder', 'bivariate'}, default='householder') – Type of the oblique directions.

  • max_oblique_features (int, default=2) – Householder only: maximum number of pivots combined by a direction.

  • tau (float, default=1e-4) – Householder only: threshold of the reflection.

  • n_orientations (int, default=10) – Bivariate only: number of angles.

Variables:

  • numerical (list of int) – Numerical columns of the feature matrix.

  • categorical (list of int) – Categorical columns of the feature matrix (none).

  • candidates (numpy.ndarray of shape (n_candidates, n_numerical_features)) – The candidate pivots, standardized.

  • candidate_names (numpy.ndarray of shape (n_candidates,)) – Indices of the candidate pivots in the training set.

  • oblique (HouseholderTransformer or BivariateTransformer) – The oblique transformer of the distances from the candidates.

  • pivots (dict) – Indices in the training set of the descriptives, discriminatives and candidates pivots of the node.

  • scaler (StandardScaler) – Scaler of the numerical features, fitted on the whole data set.

class HouseholderTransformer(max_oblique_features=2, tau=0.0001, random_state=None)[source]

Bases: ObliqueTransformer

Transformer into the oblique directions given by a Householder reflection: the reflection that maps the principal component of the instances of the node onto the closest axis.

The reflected axes H = I - 2 u u^T are the columns of the feature matrix: the splitter chooses the best threshold on one of them. The reflection vector u is restricted to the max_oblique_features features with the largest weights, so that each oblique direction combines at most that many features (plus the axis itself); the axes not involved in the reflection stay axis-parallel. The directions are computed on the numerical features of the node, as they are: scale the data beforehand if the features have different scales.

Parameters:

  • max_oblique_features (int, default=2) – Maximum number of features in the reflection vector.

  • tau (float, default=1e-4) – If the principal component is closer than tau to every axis, the directions are the axes (no reflection).

class ObliquePivotTransformer(ml_task='clf', distance_measure='euclidean', k=None, reg_contrast_lambda=0.2, reg_max_thresholds=None, tree_params=None, oblique_split_type='householder', max_oblique_features=2, tau=0.0001, n_orientations=10)[source]

Bases: PivotTransformer

Transformer of tabular data into oblique directions on the distances from the candidate pivots, e.g. 0.7 * P_12 - 0.3 * P_57 where P_i is the distance from the training instance i.

The candidate pivots are selected as in PivotTransformer, but the discriminative ones are the instances combined by an oblique direction (oblique_split_type, see PivotSelection). Then the distances of the instances of the node from the candidates are transformed by an oblique transformer of the same type (HouseholderTransformer or BivariateTransformer), whose directions are the columns of the feature matrix. After the splitter has chosen a column, only its direction is kept (select); the candidates are all kept.

Parameters:

  • ml_task ({MODEL_TYPE_CLF, MODEL_TYPE_REG}, default=MODEL_TYPE_CLF) – Task of the stump, which determines how the pivots and the directions are selected.

  • distance_measure (str or callable, default='euclidean') – Distance between the instances, computed on the standardized numerical features.

  • k – Regression only: see PivotTransformer.

  • reg_contrast_lambda – Regression only: see PivotTransformer.

  • reg_max_thresholds – Regression only: see PivotTransformer.

  • tree_params (dict, default=None) – Parameters of the decision trees that select the pivots and the directions.

  • oblique_split_type ({'householder', 'bivariate'}, default='householder') – Type of the oblique directions.

  • max_oblique_features (int, default=2) – Householder only: maximum number of pivots combined by a direction.

  • tau (float, default=1e-4) – Householder only: threshold of the reflection.

  • n_orientations (int, default=10) – Bivariate only: number of angles.

Variables:

  • numerical (list of int) – Numerical columns of the feature matrix.

  • categorical (list of int) – Categorical columns of the feature matrix (none).

  • candidates (numpy.ndarray of shape (n_candidates, n_numerical_features)) – The candidate pivots, standardized.

  • candidate_names (numpy.ndarray of shape (n_candidates,)) – Indices of the candidate pivots in the training set.

  • oblique (HouseholderTransformer or BivariateTransformer) – The oblique transformer of the distances from the candidates.

  • pivots (dict) – Indices in the training set of the descriptives, discriminatives and candidates pivots of the node.

  • scaler (StandardScaler) – Scaler of the numerical features, fitted on the whole data set.
