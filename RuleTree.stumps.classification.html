DecisionTreeStumpClassifier: the split on one feature for classification.

class DecisionTreeStumpClassifier(criterion='gini', min_samples_leaf=1, min_weight_fraction_leaf=0.0, max_features=None, random_state=None, min_impurity_decrease=0.0, class_weight=None)[source]#

Bases: Stump

Decision tree stump for classification: a threshold on a numerical feature (x <= t) or a value of a categorical feature (x == v), chosen to maximize the information gain.

It works on tabular data, time series and images; time series and images are flattened into one feature per value. It is a Stump made of a MatrixTransformer and a ClassificationSplitter, and the default stump of RuleTreeClassifier.

Parameters:

  • criterion ({'gini', 'entropy'}, default='gini') – Impurity measure.

  • min_samples_leaf (int or float, default=1) – Minimum number of instances in each child (a fraction of the instances of the node if float). Applies to the numerical features.

  • min_weight_fraction_leaf (float, default=0.0) – Minimum fraction of the sample weights of the node in each child. Applies to the numerical features.

  • max_features (int, float, {'sqrt', 'log2'} or None, default=None) – Number of numerical features considered for the split, drawn at random; None for all.

  • random_state (int, RandomState instance or None, default=None) – Seed of the choice of the features (max_features) and of the ties between features with the same gain. With None, a tree gives the stump its own random_state.

  • min_impurity_decrease (float, default=0.0) – A numerical split is kept only if it decreases the weighted impurity by at least this value.

  • class_weight (dict, 'balanced' or None, default=None) – Weights of the classes in the impurity; ‘balanced’ weighs them inversely to their frequency.

Variables:

  • transformer (MatrixTransformer) – The fitted transformer.

  • splitter (ClassificationSplitter) – The fitted splitter (feature, threshold, gain and impurity of the split).

  • data_type (str) – Data type the stump was fitted on.

  • feature_original (int) – Column of the data used by the split.

  • threshold_original (float or object) – Threshold of a numerical split, or value of a categorical split.

See also

DecisionTreeStumpRegressor

the same stump for regression.

sklearn.tree.DecisionTreeClassifier

the tree used to split the numerical features.

Examples

>>> from sklearn.datasets import load_iris
>>> from RuleTree import RuleTreeClassifier
>>> from RuleTree.stumps.classification import DecisionTreeStumpClassifier
>>> X, y = load_iris(return_X_y=True)
>>> stump = DecisionTreeStumpClassifier(criterion="entropy", random_state=0).fit(X, y)
>>> stump.get_rule()["textual_rule"]
'X_3 <= 0.8'
>>> stump.apply(X[[0, 100]])  # 1: left child, 2: right child
array([1, 2])

As base stump of a tree:

>>> model = RuleTreeClassifier(max_depth=2, base_stumps=DecisionTreeStumpClassifier(min_samples_leaf=5))
>>> model = model.fit(X, y)

Bases: MultiplePivotTreeStump, DecisionTreeStumpClassifier

Multiple oblique pivot stump for classification of tabular data: as MultiplePivotTreeStumpClassifier (the instances closer to one pivot than to another one go left, P_a <= P_b), but the discriminative pivots of each class are the instances combined by an oblique direction on the distances (oblique_split_type), instead of the root split of a decision tree: up to max_oblique_features (Householder) or two (bivariate) for each class.

It is a Stump made of a MultiplePivotTransformer and a ClassificationSplitter.

  • max_oblique_features (int, default=2) – Householder only: maximum number of pivots combined by a direction.

  • tau (float, default=1e-4) – Householder only: if the principal component is closer than tau to every axis, the directions are the axes.

  • n_orientations (int, default=10) – Bivariate only: number of angles, evenly spaced in [0, 180) degrees.

  • criterion ({'gini', 'entropy'}, default='gini') – Impurity measure.

  • min_weight_fraction_leaf (float, default=0.0) – Minimum fraction of the sample weights of the node in each child. Applies to the numerical features.

  • max_features (int, float, {'sqrt', 'log2'} or None, default=None) – Number of numerical features considered for the split, drawn at random; None for all.

  • random_state (int, RandomState instance or None, default=None) – Seed of the choice of the features (max_features) and of the ties between features with the same gain. With None, a tree gives the stump its own random_state.

  • min_impurity_decrease (float, default=0.0) – A numerical split is kept only if it decreases the weighted impurity by at least this value.

  • class_weight (dict, 'balanced' or None, default=None) – Weights of the classes in the impurity; ‘balanced’ weighs them inversely to their frequency.

Variables:

  • transformer (MultiplePivotTransformer) – The fitted transformer.

  • splitter (ClassificationSplitter) – The fitted splitter (feature, threshold, gain and impurity of the split).

  • data_type (str) – Data type the stump was fitted on.

MultiplePivotTreeStumpClassifier: which of two pivots is closer to the instance, for classification.

class MultiplePivotTreeStumpClassifier(distance_measure='euclidean', criterion='gini', min_samples_leaf=1, min_weight_fraction_leaf=0.0, max_features=None, random_state=None, min_impurity_decrease=0.0, class_weight=None)[source]#

Bases: MultiplePivotTreeStump, DecisionTreeStumpClassifier

Multiple pivot stump for classification of tabular data: the instances closer to one pivot than to another one go left (P_a <= P_b, where P_i is the distance from the training instance i).

At each node, the candidate pivots are selected as in PivotTreeStumpClassifier (for each class, its medoid and its most discriminative instance), and the split is the pair of pivots of different classes whose comparison best splits the node. Distances are computed on the standardized numerical features; the distance matrix of the training set is computed once and cached in the tree.

It is a Stump made of a MultiplePivotTransformer and a ClassificationSplitter.

Parameters:

  • distance_measure (str or callable, default='euclidean') – Distance between the instances, computed on the standardized numerical features: any metric of sklearn.metrics.pairwise_distances.

  • criterion ({'gini', 'entropy'}, default='gini') – Impurity measure.

  • min_samples_leaf (int or float, default=1) – Minimum number of instances in each child (a fraction of the instances of the node if float). Applies to the numerical features.

  • min_weight_fraction_leaf (float, default=0.0) – Minimum fraction of the sample weights of the node in each child. Applies to the numerical features.

  • max_features (int, float, {'sqrt', 'log2'} or None, default=None) – Number of numerical features considered for the split, drawn at random; None for all.

  • random_state (int, RandomState instance or None, default=None) – Seed of the choice of the features (max_features) and of the ties between features with the same gain. With None, a tree gives the stump its own random_state.

  • min_impurity_decrease (float, default=0.0) – A numerical split is kept only if it decreases the weighted impurity by at least this value.

  • class_weight (dict, 'balanced' or None, default=None) – Weights of the classes in the impurity; ‘balanced’ weighs them inversely to their frequency.

Variables:

  • transformer (MultiplePivotTransformer) – The fitted transformer.

  • splitter (ClassificationSplitter) – The fitted splitter (feature, threshold, gain and impurity of the split).

  • data_type (str) – Data type the stump was fitted on.

  • feature_original (tuple of int) – Indices (in the training set) of the two pivots of the split.

RuleTree.stumps.classification.ObliqueDecisionTreeStumpClassifier module#

ObliqueDecisionTreeStumpClassifier: a threshold on a linear combination of features, for classification.

class ObliqueDecisionTreeStumpClassifier(oblique_split_type='householder', max_oblique_features=2, tau=0.0001, n_orientations=10, criterion='gini', min_samples_leaf=1, min_weight_fraction_leaf=0.0, max_features=None, random_state=None, min_impurity_decrease=0.0, class_weight=None)[source]#

Bases: ObliqueTreeStump, DecisionTreeStumpClassifier

Oblique stump for classification of tabular data: a threshold on a linear combination of a few numerical features (0.8 * X_2 - 0.6 * X_3 <= t goes left), i.e. a hyperplane that is not parallel to the axes.

The directions are chosen at each node in one of two ways (oblique_split_type):

  • 'householder': the axes reflected by the Householder reflection that maps the principal component of the node onto an axis; each direction combines at most max_oblique_features features (see HouseholderTransformer);

  • 'bivariate': n_orientations rotations of the best pair of features (see BivariateTransformer).

The combinations are computed on the numerical features as they are: scale the data beforehand if the features have different scales. It is a Stump made of an oblique transformer and a ClassificationSplitter.

Parameters:

  • oblique_split_type ({'householder', 'bivariate'}, default='householder') – How the oblique directions are chosen.

  • max_oblique_features (int, default=2) – Householder only: maximum number of features in the reflection vector.

  • tau (float, default=1e-4) – Householder only: if the principal component is closer than tau to every axis, the directions are the axes.

  • n_orientations (int, default=10) – Bivariate only: number of angles, evenly spaced in [0, 180) degrees.

  • criterion ({'gini', 'entropy'}, default='gini') – Impurity measure.

  • min_samples_leaf (int or float, default=1) – Minimum number of instances in each child (a fraction of the instances of the node if float).

  • min_weight_fraction_leaf (float, default=0.0) – Minimum fraction of the sample weights of the node in each child.

  • max_features (int, float, {'sqrt', 'log2'} or None, default=None) – Number of directions considered for the split, drawn at random; None for all.

  • random_state (int, RandomState instance or None, default=None) – Seed of the PCA (Householder), of the choice of the directions (max_features) and of the ties between directions with the same gain. With None, a tree gives the stump its own random_state.

  • min_impurity_decrease (float, default=0.0) – A split is kept only if it decreases the weighted impurity by at least this value.

  • class_weight (dict, 'balanced' or None, default=None) – Weights of the classes in the impurity; ‘balanced’ weighs them inversely to their frequency.

Variables:

  • transformer (HouseholderTransformer or BivariateTransformer) – The fitted transformer.

  • splitter (ClassificationSplitter) – The fitted splitter (feature, threshold, gain and impurity of the split).

  • data_type (str) – Data type the stump was fitted on.

  • feature_original (tuple of int) – Columns of the data combined by the split.

  • coefficients (list of float) – Their coefficients.

  • threshold_original (float) – Threshold of the split.

See also

Pivot stump for classification: a threshold on the distance from a pivot, an instance of the training set (distance <= t goes left).

At each node, the candidate pivots are selected among the instances of the node: for each class, its medoid (descriptive pivot) and the instance whose distance best separates the classes (discriminative pivot). The split is the best threshold on the distance from one of the candidates. Distances are computed on the standardized numerical features; the distance matrix of the training set is computed once and cached in the tree. In the rules, P_<i> is the distance from the training instance i.

It is a Stump made of a PivotTransformer and a ClassificationSplitter; it supports only tabular data.

At each node, n_shapelets shapelets are selected among candidate subsequences, and the split is the best threshold on the distance from one of them. In the rules, euclidean(TS, shp) is the distance from the shapelet.

It is a Stump made of a ShapeletTransformer and a ClassificationSplitter. The time series have shape (n_samples, n_signals, length) and are passed to the trees as X_ts.

Parameters:

  • n_shapelets (int, default=8) – Number of shapelets selected at each node.

  • n_shapelets_for_selection (int, numpy.inf or 'stratified', default=500) – Number of candidate subsequences extracted from the time series, among which the shapelets are selected. ‘stratified’ draws them according to the class distribution.

  • n_ts_for_selection_per_class (int or numpy.inf, default=100) – Number of time series of each class from which the candidates are extracted.

  • sliding_window (int, default=50) – Length of the shapelets.

  • selection ({'random', 'mi_clf', 'cluster'}, default='mi_clf') – How the shapelets are selected among the candidates: at random, by mutual information with the target, or as the medoids of a k-medoids clustering (requires scikit-learn-extra).

  • distance ({'euclidean', 'sqeuclidean', 'cosine', 'cityblock'} or callable, default='euclidean') – Distance between a shapelet and a subsequence; the distance of a time series from a shapelet is the minimum over the positions of the shapelet.

  • mi_n_neighbors (int, default=100) – Number of neighbors used to estimate the mutual information.

  • random_state (int, default=42) – Seed of the random choices of the candidates and of the shapelets.

  • n_jobs (int, default=1) – Number of threads used to compute the distances (-1: all the CPUs).

  • criterion ({'gini', 'entropy'}, default='gini') – Impurity measure.

  • min_samples_leaf (int or float, default=1) – Minimum number of instances in each child (a fraction of the instances of the node if float). Applies to the numerical features.

  • min_weight_fraction_leaf (float, default=0.0) – Minimum fraction of the sample weights of the node in each child. Applies to the numerical features.

  • max_features (int, float, {'sqrt', 'log2'} or None, default=None) – Number of numerical features considered for the split, drawn at random; None for all.

  • min_impurity_decrease (float, default=0.0) – A numerical split is kept only if it decreases the weighted impurity by at least this value.

  • class_weight (dict, 'balanced' or None, default=None) – Weights of the classes in the impurity; ‘balanced’ weighs them inversely to their frequency.

Variables:

  • transformer (ShapeletTransformer) – The fitted transformer.

  • splitter (ClassificationSplitter) – The fitted splitter (feature, threshold, gain and impurity of the split).

  • data_type (str) – Data type the stump was fitted on.

  • feature_original (int) – Index of the shapelet of the split among the selected ones.

  • threshold_original (float) – Threshold of the split.

Oblique stump for classification of tabular data: a threshold on a linear combination of a few numerical features (0.8 * X_2 - 0.6 * X_3 <= t goes left), i.e. a hyperplane that is not parallel to the axes.

The directions are chosen at each node in one of two ways (oblique_split_type):

  • 'householder': the axes reflected by the Householder reflection that maps the principal component of the node onto an axis; each direction combines at most max_oblique_features features (see HouseholderTransformer);

  • 'bivariate': n_orientations rotations of the best pair of features (see BivariateTransformer).

The combinations are computed on the numerical features as they are: scale the data beforehand if the features have different scales. It is a Stump made of an oblique transformer and a ClassificationSplitter.

Parameters:

  • oblique_split_type ({'householder', 'bivariate'}, default='householder') – How the oblique directions are chosen.

  • max_oblique_features (int, default=2) – Householder only: maximum number of features in the reflection vector.

  • tau (float, default=1e-4) – Householder only: if the principal component is closer than tau to every axis, the directions are the axes.

  • n_orientations (int, default=10) – Bivariate only: number of angles, evenly spaced in [0, 180) degrees.

  • criterion ({'gini', 'entropy'}, default='gini') – Impurity measure.

  • min_samples_leaf (int or float, default=1) – Minimum number of instances in each child (a fraction of the instances of the node if float).

  • min_weight_fraction_leaf (float, default=0.0) – Minimum fraction of the sample weights of the node in each child.

  • max_features (int, float, {'sqrt', 'log2'} or None, default=None) – Number of directions considered for the split, drawn at random; None for all.

  • random_state (int, RandomState instance or None, default=None) – Seed of the PCA (Householder), of the choice of the directions (max_features) and of the ties between directions with the same gain. With None, a tree gives the stump its own random_state.

  • min_impurity_decrease (float, default=0.0) – A split is kept only if it decreases the weighted impurity by at least this value.

  • class_weight (dict, 'balanced' or None, default=None) – Weights of the classes in the impurity; ‘balanced’ weighs them inversely to their frequency.

Variables:

  • splitter (ClassificationSplitter) – The fitted splitter (feature, threshold, gain and impurity of the split).

  • data_type (str) – Data type the stump was fitted on.

  • feature_original (tuple of int) – Columns of the data combined by the split.

Partial proximity stump for classification of tabular data: the instances closer to one partial pivot than to another one go left (euclidean(x, SP0(...)) <= euclidean(x, SP1(...))).

At each node, n_shapelets partial pivots (instances of the node restricted to a few features) are selected as in PartialPivotTreeStumpClassifier, and the split is the pair of pivots whose comparison best splits the node. In the rules, SP<i>(...) lists the features and values of the pivot.

It is a Stump made of a PartialProximityTransformer and a ClassificationSplitter.

Parameters:

  • n_shapelets (int or numpy.inf, default=8) – Number of partial pivots selected at each node (numpy.inf with selection=’all’).

  • n_ts_for_selection (int, float or numpy.inf, default=100) – Number (int) or fraction (float) of the instances of the node used to select the pivots.

  • n_features_strategy (int, float, tuple or str, default=2) – Number of features of each partial pivot: an int, a fraction of the features (float), a range (min, max), or chosen from the feature importances of a random forest (‘elbow’, ‘drop’, ‘new’) or iteratively with depth-1 trees (‘iterative’).

  • selection ({'random', 'cluster', 'all'}, default='random') – How the pivots are selected among the candidates: at random, the instances closest to the k-means centroids, or all of them.

  • distance ({'euclidean', 'sqeuclidean', 'cosine', 'cityblock'} or callable, default='euclidean') – Distance between an instance and a partial pivot, on the features of the pivot. The data are used as they are: scale them beforehand if the features have different scales.

  • proximity_on_same_features (bool, default=True) – Whether to compare only the partial pivots on the same features, whose distances are comparable.

  • use_combination (bool, default=True) – With the strategies based on feature importances, whether to use all the combinations of the important features or only the set of all of them.

  • random_state (int, default=42) – Seed of the random choices.

  • n_jobs (int, default=1) – Number of threads used to compute the distances (-1: all the CPUs).

  • criterion ({'gini', 'entropy'}, default='gini') – Impurity measure.

  • min_samples_leaf (int or float, default=1) – Minimum number of instances in each child (a fraction of the instances of the node if float). Applies to the numerical features.

  • min_weight_fraction_leaf (float, default=0.0) – Minimum fraction of the sample weights of the node in each child. Applies to the numerical features.

  • max_features (int, float, {'sqrt', 'log2'} or None, default=None) – Number of numerical features considered for the split, drawn at random; None for all.

  • min_impurity_decrease (float, default=0.0) – A numerical split is kept only if it decreases the weighted impurity by at least this value.

  • class_weight (dict, 'balanced' or None, default=None) – Weights of the classes in the impurity; ‘balanced’ weighs them inversely to their frequency.

Variables:

  • transformer (PartialProximityTransformer) – The fitted transformer.

  • splitter (ClassificationSplitter) – The fitted splitter (feature, threshold, gain and impurity of the split).

  • data_type (str) – Data type the stump was fitted on.

  • st (TabularShapelets) – The partial pivots of the split: st.shapelets[0] and st.shapelets[1].

Bases: MultiplePivotTreeStump, DecisionTreeStumpClassifier

Multiple pivot stump for classification of tabular data: the instances closer to one pivot than to another one go left (P_a <= P_b, where P_i is the distance from the training instance i).

At each node, the candidate pivots are selected as in PivotTreeStumpClassifier (for each class, its medoid and its most discriminative instance), and the split is the pair of pivots of different classes whose comparison best splits the node. Distances are computed on the standardized numerical features; the distance matrix of the training set is computed once and cached in the tree.

It is a Stump made of a MultiplePivotTransformer and a ClassificationSplitter.

Parameters:

  • distance_measure (str or callable, default='euclidean') – Distance between the instances, computed on the standardized numerical features: any metric of sklearn.metrics.pairwise_distances.

  • criterion ({'gini', 'entropy'}, default='gini') – Impurity measure.

  • min_samples_leaf (int or float, default=1) – Minimum number of instances in each child (a fraction of the instances of the node if float). Applies to the numerical features.

  • min_weight_fraction_leaf (float, default=0.0) – Minimum fraction of the sample weights of the node in each child. Applies to the numerical features.

  • max_features (int, float, {'sqrt', 'log2'} or None, default=None) – Number of numerical features considered for the split, drawn at random; None for all.

  • random_state (int, RandomState instance or None, default=None) – Seed of the choice of the features (max_features) and of the ties between features with the same gain. With None, a tree gives the stump its own random_state.

  • min_impurity_decrease (float, default=0.0) – A numerical split is kept only if it decreases the weighted impurity by at least this value.

  • class_weight (dict, 'balanced' or None, default=None) – Weights of the classes in the impurity; ‘balanced’ weighs them inversely to their frequency.

Variables:

  • transformer (MultiplePivotTransformer) – The fitted transformer.

  • splitter (ClassificationSplitter) – The fitted splitter (feature, threshold, gain and impurity of the split).

  • feature_original (tuple of int) – Indices (in the training set) of the two pivots of the split.

Bases: ObliquePivotTreeStump, DecisionTreeStumpClassifier

Oblique pivot stump for classification of tabular data: a threshold on a linear combination of the distances from a few pivots (0.7 * P_12 - 0.3 * P_57 <= t goes left, where P_i is the distance from the training instance i).

At each node, the candidate pivots are, for each class, its medoid and the instances combined by an oblique direction on the distances (oblique_split_type); then the distances of the instances of the node from the candidates are combined by oblique directions of the same type, and the split is the best threshold on one of them (see ObliquePivotTransformer). Distances are computed on the standardized numerical features.

It is a Stump made of an ObliquePivotTransformer and a ClassificationSplitter.

Parameters:

  • distance_measure (str or callable, default='euclidean') – Distance between the instances, computed on the standardized numerical features: any metric of sklearn.metrics.pairwise_distances.

  • oblique_split_type ({'householder', 'bivariate'}, default='householder') – How the oblique directions are chosen (see ObliqueDecisionTreeStumpClassifier).

  • max_oblique_features (int, default=2) – Householder only: maximum number of pivots combined by a direction.

  • tau (float, default=1e-4) – Householder only: if the principal component is closer than tau to every axis, the directions are the axes.

  • n_orientations (int, default=10) – Bivariate only: number of angles, evenly spaced in [0, 180) degrees.

  • criterion ({'gini', 'entropy'}, default='gini') – Impurity measure.

  • min_samples_leaf (int or float, default=1) – Minimum number of instances in each child (a fraction of the instances of the node if float). Applies to the numerical features.

  • min_weight_fraction_leaf (float, default=0.0) – Minimum fraction of the sample weights of the node in each child. Applies to the numerical features.

  • max_features (int, float, {'sqrt', 'log2'} or None, default=None) – Number of numerical features considered for the split, drawn at random; None for all.

  • random_state (int, RandomState instance or None, default=None) – Seed of the choice of the features (max_features) and of the ties between features with the same gain. With None, a tree gives the stump its own random_state.

  • min_impurity_decrease (float, default=0.0) – A numerical split is kept only if it decreases the weighted impurity by at least this value.

  • class_weight (dict, 'balanced' or None, default=None) – Weights of the classes in the impurity; ‘balanced’ weighs them inversely to their frequency.

Variables:

  • transformer (ObliquePivotTransformer) – The fitted transformer.

  • splitter (ClassificationSplitter) – The fitted splitter (feature, threshold, gain and impurity of the split).

  • data_type (str) – Data type the stump was fitted on.

  • feature_original (tuple of int) – Indices (in the training set) of the pivots combined by the split.

  • coefficients (list of float) – Their coefficients.
